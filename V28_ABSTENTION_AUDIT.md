## Interpretation limits

This is an AI-assisted design audit, not a human legal adjudication. One strong
independent model classified answerability from the four summarized input
blocks; the ground truth was revealed only in a second step. The classifications
therefore identify hypotheses for the v29 gate rather than replacing a legal
review. In particular, “safe direction” means that the summarized record can
support a directional mediation option; it does not mean that an exact amount
or enforceable final agreement can always be produced.
